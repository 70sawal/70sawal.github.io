RTI बैंक

कोई भी भारतीय नागरिक RTI डाल सकता है. फ़ीस सिर्फ़ ₹10. जवाब 30 दिन में आना अनिवार्य है. नीचे दिए गए आवेदन कॉपी-पेस्ट करके भेजें, और जवाब आने पर हमें भेजें. जाँच के बाद, नाम और पता हटाकर, प्रकाशित करेंगे.

RTI compels disclosure of RECORDS THE DEPARTMENT HOLDS. It does not compel opinions, justifications, or answers to 'why' questions, those get rejected under Section 2(f). Every application below is worded to ask only for records, numbers, copies and dates.

RTI-01, पेपर लीक

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Home Department / Uttarakhand Police Headquarters, Dehradun

  1. The total number of FIRs registered under the Uttarakhand Competitive Examination (Measures for Control and Prevention of Unfair Means in Recruitment) Act/Ordinance, 2023, from the date it came into force to the date of this application, year-wise.
  2. Of those FIRs, the number in which a charge sheet has been filed, year-wise.
  3. Of those, the number in which trial has concluded, and the number resulting in conviction and in acquittal respectively.
  4. The total quantum of fines imposed and actually recovered under the said Act to date.
  5. Copies of any departmental circulars or standard operating procedures issued for the prevention of question paper leaks since 1 January 2023.
RTI-02, रोज़गार

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC), Dehradun, and separately, Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC), Haridwar

  1. The number of posts advertised by this Commission in each financial year from 2017-18 to the current year.
  2. For each such advertisement: the date of the examination as originally scheduled, the date on which it was actually held, and whether it was cancelled, postponed or re-conducted.
  3. The number of candidates who applied and the total examination fee collected, in each financial year from 2017-18 onward.
  4. The number of candidates who were issued appointment letters as a result of each recruitment process, year-wise.
  5. Copies of any orders providing for refund of examination fees to candidates in respect of examinations that were cancelled.
RTI-03, पलायन

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Rural Development and Migration Prevention Commission (Palayan Nivaran Aayog), Pauri

  1. The number of villages recorded as uninhabited in the State, as per the latest survey conducted by this Commission, district-wise, along with the date of that survey.
  2. Copies of all interim and final reports submitted by this Commission to the State Government since its constitution.
  3. A list of the recommendations made by this Commission since 2017, and for each, the current implementation status as recorded in the Commission's files.
  4. The budget allocated to and expenditure incurred by this Commission in each financial year since its constitution.
  5. The number of families recorded as having returned to their villages (reverse migration) under any State scheme, year-wise.
RTI-04, पलायन

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Directorate of Education (Basic & Secondary), Dehradun, and separately, Directorate of Medical Health & Family Welfare, Dehradun

  1. The number of government schools opened, and the number closed or merged, in each of the hill districts, year-wise from 2017-18 to date.
  2. The number of sanctioned teaching posts and the number currently vacant, district-wise.
  3. (To Health) The number of Primary Health Centres and Community Health Centres functional in each hill district, and the number of sanctioned doctor posts currently vacant in each.
  4. The number of PHCs/CHCs in hill districts currently operating without a resident doctor.
RTI-05, भू-क़ानून

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Revenue Department, Government of Uttarakhand, Dehradun

  1. The total area of agricultural and non-agricultural land permitted to be purchased by persons not domiciled in Uttarakhand, under permissions granted by the State Government, in each year from 2017 to date, district-wise.
  2. The number of such permissions granted, and the number rejected, year-wise.
  3. The number of cases in which land purchased under such permission was found to have been used for a purpose other than the one stated in the application.
  4. Of those cases, the number in which the land was vested in the State, and the total area so vested, following the coming into force of the 2025 land law.
RTI-06, विधायक का हिसाब

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Uttarakhand Vidhan Sabha Secretariat, Dehradun

  1. The number of sitting days of the Uttarakhand Vidhan Sabha in each year from 2017 to date.
  2. Member-wise attendance records for each session held since March 2017.
  3. The number of starred and unstarred questions tabled by each Member in each session since March 2017.
  4. The number of questions listed for reply that were not answered within the stipulated period, session-wise.
  5. The total expenditure incurred on each session, session-wise.
RTI-07, वादा vs हक़ीक़त

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Department of Women Empowerment & Child Development, Dehradun, and separately, Social Welfare Department, and Food & Civil Supplies Department, Government of Uttarakhand

  1. The name of the scheme, if any, under which a monthly payment of Rs 2,000 is made to women heads of Below Poverty Line families in Uttarakhand, together with a copy of the government order sanctioning it.
  2. The number of beneficiaries who have received that payment, year-wise since 2022-23, and the total amount disbursed.
  3. The name of the scheme, if any, under which Rs 40,000 is paid to pregnant women residing in hill regions, the number of beneficiaries, and the total amount disbursed, year-wise since 2022-23.
  4. (To Food & Civil Supplies) The number of households provided free LPG cylinders, and the number of cylinders provided per household, year-wise since 2022-23.
  5. (To Social Welfare) The rate of old-age pension in force in each year since 2022-23, together with copies of the orders revising it.
  6. The budget allocated to and expenditure incurred on horticulture development and on dairy development respectively, year-wise since 2022-23.
RTI-08, जंगल

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Forest Department (Van Vibhag), Government of Uttarakhand, Dehradun

  1. The number of forest fire incidents recorded in the State, district-wise, in each fire season since 2017.
  2. The total forest area affected by fire, district-wise, in each of those years.
  3. The number of fire watchers engaged in each fire season since 2017, and the number engaged on a daily-wage or contractual basis.
  4. The amount of wages outstanding to fire watchers as on the last day of each financial year since 2017.
  5. A list of the protective equipment issued to fire watchers, and the number of units issued, in each of those years.
  6. The number of deaths and injuries of forest department personnel and of members of the public in forest fires, year-wise since 2017.
RTI-09, जंगल

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority (CAMPA), Uttarakhand, Dehradun, and separately, Principal Chief Conservator of Forests, Uttarakhand

  1. The total CAMPA funds received by the State and the total expenditure incurred, year-wise since 2017-18.
  2. A list of the activities on which CAMPA funds were spent in each year, with the amount against each activity head.
  3. Copies of any audit objections, para-wise, raised on the utilisation of CAMPA funds since 2017-18, together with the departmental replies furnished to each.
  4. The action taken on the findings of CAG Report No. 5 of 2024 regarding diversion of Rs 13.86 crore to inadmissible activities, including the amount recovered, if any, and from whom.
  5. The area on which compensatory afforestation was carried out and the survival rate of planted saplings recorded in each year since 2017-18, division-wise.
  6. The number of proposals for diversion of forest land approved in the State, year-wise since 2017, and the area of forest land diverted under each.
  7. Copies of the conditions imposed in each such approval, and the record of compliance verification carried out against those conditions.
RTI-10, महिला सुरक्षा

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Uttarakhand Police Headquarters, Dehradun, and separately, Department of Women Empowerment & Child Development

  1. The number of cases registered under crimes against women in the State, district-wise, in each year since 2017, and the number in which a charge sheet was filed.
  2. The number of such cases in which trial concluded, and the number resulting in conviction, year-wise since 2017.
  3. The number of women's help desks and women's police stations functional in the State, district-wise, and the sanctioned versus filled strength of women police personnel.
  4. The number of live-in relationships registered under the Uniform Civil Code since it came into force, month-wise, and the number of registrations of termination.
  5. The number of prosecutions initiated for failure to register a live-in relationship under the said Code.
  6. The number of hotels, resorts, dhabas and homestays inspected for compliance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, year-wise since 2017, and the number found non-compliant.
RTI-11, यात्रा

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Department of Tourism, Government of Uttarakhand, Dehradun, and separately, the District Magistrates of Rudraprayag, Chamoli and Uttarkashi

  1. Copies of any carrying-capacity study commissioned or received by the Department in respect of the Char Dham yatra routes and destination towns, together with the date of each.
  2. The number of pilgrims recorded at each of the four dhams, year-wise since 2017.
  3. The number of deaths recorded during the Char Dham yatra, year-wise since 2017, with cause of death where recorded.
  4. The number of additional doctors, ambulances and temporary medical posts deployed on the yatra routes in each year since 2017, and the peak-week deployment separately.
  5. The revenue accruing to the State from the yatra, year-wise since 2017, and the amount transferred to local bodies in the yatra districts.
  6. The number of registered homestays in the State, district-wise, and the number recorded as operational in the most recent year.
RTI-12, शासन

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Dehradun Smart City Limited, Dehradun, and Urban Development Department, Government of Uttarakhand

  1. The total funds received and expenditure incurred under the Smart City Mission in Dehradun, year-wise since 2018-19.
  2. A list of all projects sanctioned under the Mission, with the sanctioned cost, scheduled completion date and actual completion date of each.
  3. The action taken on each audit observation raised in CAG Report No. 5 of 2025 on Smart Cities, para-wise, together with the departmental reply furnished to each.
  4. The amount, if any, recovered from any agency or official in respect of the irregularities recorded in that report, and from whom.
  5. Copies of the payment terms agreed with the Project Management Consultant, and the total paid to that consultant, year-wise.
  6. The current operational status of the biometric and sensor based solid waste management module, and the dates on which it was commissioned and first used.
RTI-13, जंगल

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Directorate of Geology and Mining, Government of Uttarakhand, and Uttarakhand Forest Development Corporation

  1. The number of stone crusher licences granted, renewed and cancelled in the State, district-wise, year-wise since 2017.
  2. The quantity of minor minerals extracted from riverbeds, river-wise, and the royalty realised, year-wise since 2017.
  3. Copies of any orders of the National Green Tribunal or the Supreme Court communicated to the department on riverbed mining since 2017, and the compliance report filed against each.
  4. The number of cases of illegal mining detected, the penalty imposed, and the penalty actually recovered, year-wise since 2017.
  5. Copies of any agreement under which the collection of royalty or the monitoring of mining sites has been assigned to any private agency, together with the order approving it.
  6. The details of any penalty imposed on a government corporation or department for mining in violation of a court or tribunal order, and the amount recovered.
RTI-14, विधायक का हिसाब

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Directorate of Rural Development / District Magistrate's office of the relevant district, Government of Uttarakhand

  1. The total amount sanctioned under the MLA Local Area Development Scheme (Vidhayak Nidhi) for each of the 70 assembly constituencies of Uttarakhand, year by year, from financial year 2017-18 to the latest completed financial year.
  2. For each constituency and each of those years, the amount actually released, the amount actually spent, and the unspent balance carried forward.
  3. A list of works sanctioned under the scheme in the constituency named in this application, giving for each work: its description, the sanctioned amount, the amount spent, the executing agency, and whether the work is complete, incomplete or abandoned.
  4. The number and details of works sanctioned but never started, along with the reason recorded on file.
  5. Copies of the utilisation certificates submitted for the last three financial years for the constituency named in this application.
  6. The names of the constituencies with the highest and the lowest percentage utilisation of MLALAD funds in the latest completed financial year.
RTI-15, निर्माण

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Public Works Department (Lok Nirman Vibhag), Government of Uttarakhand

  1. The total length of motorable road sanctioned in Uttarakhand in each financial year from 2017-18 to the latest completed financial year, and the length actually completed and handed over in each of those years.
  2. The number of road works sanctioned in the hill districts (Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Rudraprayag, Tehri, Pauri, Pithoragarh, Bageshwar, Almora, Champawat) that remain incomplete as on the date of this application, with the date of original sanction and the revised completion date for each.
  3. For road works in those districts that have exceeded their original completion date by more than 24 months, the reason recorded on file and the cost escalation approved.
  4. The number of villages in Uttarakhand that remain unconnected by an all-weather motorable road as on the date of this application, district by district.
  5. The amount of penalty imposed on and actually recovered from contractors for delayed or defective road works during the period 2017-18 to date.
  6. Copies of any third-party quality audit reports commissioned for road works in the hill districts during this period.
RTI-16, जंगल

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Forest Department (Van Vibhag), Government of Uttarakhand

  1. The total number of trees permitted to be felled in Uttarakhand for road widening, highway and infrastructure projects in each year from 2017 to date, project by project.
  2. For each such project, the area of forest land diverted, in hectares, and the compensatory afforestation land identified against it.
  3. The number of saplings planted as compensatory afforestation in each of those years, and the number found surviving at the most recent survival survey, with the date of that survey.
  4. Copies of the survival survey reports for compensatory afforestation carried out in the last five years.
  5. The number of trees permitted to be translocated rather than felled in the last five years, the cost incurred on translocation, and the number of translocated trees found alive at the most recent inspection.
  6. Details of any project where felling was carried out before the compensatory afforestation land was identified and transferred.
RTI-17, शिक्षा

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Department of School Education / Uttarakhand Bhasha Sansthan, Government of Uttarakhand

  1. The policy, order or scheme under which Garhwali, Kumaoni and Jaunsari are taught in government schools in Uttarakhand, with copies of the relevant orders.
  2. The number of government schools in Uttarakhand in which Garhwali, Kumaoni or Jaunsari is currently taught as a subject, district by district.
  3. The number of sanctioned teaching posts for these languages, and the number currently filled.
  4. The total budget allocated and the total amount actually spent on the promotion of Garhwali, Kumaoni and Jaunsari in each financial year from 2017-18 to date.
  5. The number of textbooks published in these languages in that period, and the number of copies actually distributed to schools.
  6. Details of any committee constituted for the promotion of these languages, its members, the number of times it has met, and copies of its recommendations and the action taken on them.
RTI-18, आपदा

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Disaster Management and Rehabilitation Department, Government of Uttarakhand

  1. The total amount announced as relief or compensation for each major disaster in Uttarakhand from 2017 to date, disaster by disaster.
  2. For each of those disasters, the total amount actually disbursed to affected families as on the date of this application, and the number of families who received it.
  3. The number of families identified as affected in each of those disasters, and the number still awaiting any payment.
  4. The number of families relocated to permanent rehabilitation sites after each of those disasters, and the number still living in temporary accommodation.

RTI-19, शासन

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Secretariat Administration Department, Government of Uttarakhand

  1. A list of the Council of Ministers of Uttarakhand for each year from 2017 to date, naming each minister and the departments held by them, with the dates on which each portfolio was assigned and relinquished.
  2. The number of files pending for decision at the level of each minister as on the date of this application, department by department.
  3. The number of files that have been pending at the ministerial level for more than 90 days, department by department.
  4. The number of review meetings held by each department in each financial year from 2017-18 to date, and the number chaired by the minister in person.
  5. The total expenditure incurred on the office, travel, vehicles and residence of the Council of Ministers in each of those financial years.
  6. The number of departments that were held additionally by the Chief Minister at any point during this period, with the dates and the reason recorded on file.
RTI-20, बजट

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Finance Department, Government of Uttarakhand

  1. For each financial year from 2017-18 to the latest completed year, the budget estimate, the revised estimate and the actual expenditure for each department of the Government of Uttarakhand.
  2. The list of new schemes announced in the budget speech of each of those years, and against each, whether the scheme was actually launched, the date of launch, and the amount spent on it to date.
  3. The schemes announced in those budget speeches on which no expenditure has been incurred as on the date of this application.
  4. The total outstanding public debt of the Government of Uttarakhand at the close of each of those financial years, and the amount paid as interest in each year.

  6. The amount allocated to and actually spent in the hill districts as against the plains districts, in each of those years, if maintained in that form.
RTI-21, स्वास्थ्य

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Directorate of Medical Health and Family Welfare, Government of Uttarakhand

  1. The number of sanctioned, filled and vacant posts of doctors, specialists, nurses and paramedical staff at each Primary Health Centre, Community Health Centre and District Hospital in the nine hill districts of Uttarakhand, as on the date of this application.
  2. For each such facility, the number of staff posted there who are currently on attachment, deputation or otherwise serving elsewhere.
  3. The number of specialist doctors recruited by the Government of Uttarakhand in each year from 2017-18 to date, and the number who resigned or left service in each of those years.
  4. The number of facilities in those districts which have functioning operation theatre, blood storage and 24-hour delivery services, facility by facility.
  5. The number of patients referred out of each of those districts to Dehradun, Haldwani or outside the state in the last completed financial year.
  6. Copies of any orders providing for incentives or hardship allowance for doctors serving in remote hill postings, and the number of doctors who actually received such an allowance in the last completed year.

No reply in 30 days, or an unsatisfactory reply, triggers a First Appeal to the First Appellate Authority in the same department, free, and due within 30 days of the deadline lapsing. Send us refusals too. A refusal is itself content.

Filing an RTI puts your name and address on record with the department. Consider that before filing on politically sensitive subjects. We will never publish a filer's identity without explicit permission.
