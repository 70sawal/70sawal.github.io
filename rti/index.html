RTI बैंक

कोई भी भारतीय नागरिक RTI डाल सकता है। फीस सिर्फ़ ₹10। जवाब 30 दिन में आना अनिवार्य है। नीचे दिए गए आवेदन कॉपी-पेस्ट करके भेजें, और जवाब आने पर हमें भेजें, हम प्रकाशित करेंगे।

RTI compels disclosure of RECORDS THE DEPARTMENT HOLDS. It does not compel opinions, justifications, or answers to 'why' questions, those get rejected under Section 2(f). Every application below is worded to ask only for records, numbers, copies and dates.

RTI-01, पेपर लीक

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Home Department / Uttarakhand Police Headquarters, Dehradun

  1. 1. The total number of FIRs registered under the Uttarakhand Competitive Examination (Measures for Control and Prevention of Unfair Means in Recruitment) Act/Ordinance, 2023, from the date it came into force to the date of this application, year-wise.
  2. 2. Of those FIRs, the number in which a charge sheet has been filed, year-wise.
  3. 3. Of those, the number in which trial has concluded, and the number resulting in conviction and in acquittal respectively.
  4. 4. The total quantum of fines imposed and actually recovered under the said Act to date.
  5. 5. Copies of any departmental circulars or standard operating procedures issued for the prevention of question paper leaks since 1 January 2023.
RTI-02, रोज़गार

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC), Dehradun, and separately, Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC), Haridwar

  1. 1. The number of posts advertised by this Commission in each financial year from 2017-18 to the current year.
  2. 2. For each such advertisement: the date of the examination as originally scheduled, the date on which it was actually held, and whether it was cancelled, postponed or re-conducted.
  3. 3. The number of candidates who applied and the total examination fee collected, in each financial year from 2017-18 onward.
  4. 4. The number of candidates who were issued appointment letters as a result of each recruitment process, year-wise.
  5. 5. Copies of any orders providing for refund of examination fees to candidates in respect of examinations that were cancelled.
RTI-03, पलायन

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Rural Development and Migration Prevention Commission (Palayan Nivaran Aayog), Pauri

  1. 1. The number of villages recorded as uninhabited in the State, as per the latest survey conducted by this Commission, district-wise, along with the date of that survey.
  2. 2. Copies of all interim and final reports submitted by this Commission to the State Government since its constitution.
  3. 3. A list of the recommendations made by this Commission since 2017, and for each, the current implementation status as recorded in the Commission's files.
  4. 4. The budget allocated to and expenditure incurred by this Commission in each financial year since its constitution.
  5. 5. The number of families recorded as having returned to their villages (reverse migration) under any State scheme, year-wise.
RTI-04, पलायन

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Directorate of Education (Basic & Secondary), Dehradun, and separately, Directorate of Medical Health & Family Welfare, Dehradun

  1. 1. The number of government schools opened, and the number closed or merged, in each of the hill districts, year-wise from 2017-18 to date.
  2. 2. The number of sanctioned teaching posts and the number currently vacant, district-wise.
  3. 3. (To Health) The number of Primary Health Centres and Community Health Centres functional in each hill district, and the number of sanctioned doctor posts currently vacant in each.
  4. 4. The number of PHCs/CHCs in hill districts currently operating without a resident doctor.
RTI-05, भू-कानून

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Revenue Department, Government of Uttarakhand, Dehradun

  1. 1. The total area of agricultural and non-agricultural land permitted to be purchased by persons not domiciled in Uttarakhand, under permissions granted by the State Government, in each year from 2017 to date, district-wise.
  2. 2. The number of such permissions granted, and the number rejected, year-wise.
  3. 3. The number of cases in which land purchased under such permission was found to have been used for a purpose other than the one stated in the application.
  4. 4. Of those cases, the number in which the land was vested in the State, and the total area so vested, following the coming into force of the 2025 land law.
RTI-06, विधायक का हिसाब

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Uttarakhand Vidhan Sabha Secretariat, Dehradun

  1. 1. The number of sitting days of the Uttarakhand Vidhan Sabha in each year from 2017 to date.
  2. 2. Member-wise attendance records for each session held since March 2017.
  3. 3. The number of starred and unstarred questions tabled by each Member in each session since March 2017.
  4. 4. The number of questions listed for reply that were not answered within the stipulated period, session-wise.
  5. 5. The total expenditure incurred on each session, session-wise.
RTI-07, वादा vs हक़ीक़त

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Department of Women Empowerment & Child Development, Dehradun, and separately, Social Welfare Department, and Food & Civil Supplies Department, Government of Uttarakhand

  1. 1. The name of the scheme, if any, under which a monthly payment of Rs 2,000 is made to women heads of Below Poverty Line families in Uttarakhand, together with a copy of the government order sanctioning it.
  2. 2. The number of beneficiaries who have received that payment, year-wise since 2022-23, and the total amount disbursed.
  3. 3. The name of the scheme, if any, under which Rs 40,000 is paid to pregnant women residing in hill regions, the number of beneficiaries, and the total amount disbursed, year-wise since 2022-23.
  4. 4. (To Food & Civil Supplies) The number of households provided free LPG cylinders, and the number of cylinders provided per household, year-wise since 2022-23.
  5. 5. (To Social Welfare) The rate of old-age pension in force in each year since 2022-23, together with copies of the orders revising it.
  6. 6. The budget allocated to and expenditure incurred on horticulture development and on dairy development respectively, year-wise since 2022-23.
RTI-08, जंगल

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Forest Department (Van Vibhag), Government of Uttarakhand, Dehradun

  1. 1. The number of forest fire incidents recorded in the State, district-wise, in each fire season since 2017.
  2. 2. The total forest area affected by fire, district-wise, in each of those years.
  3. 3. The number of fire watchers engaged in each fire season since 2017, and the number engaged on a daily-wage or contractual basis.
  4. 4. The amount of wages outstanding to fire watchers as on the last day of each financial year since 2017.
  5. 5. A list of the protective equipment issued to fire watchers, and the number of units issued, in each of those years.
  6. 6. The number of deaths and injuries of forest department personnel and of members of the public in forest fires, year-wise since 2017.
RTI-09, जंगल

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority (CAMPA), Uttarakhand, Dehradun, and separately, Principal Chief Conservator of Forests, Uttarakhand

  1. 1. The total CAMPA funds received by the State and the total expenditure incurred, year-wise since 2017-18.
  2. 2. A list of the activities on which CAMPA funds were spent in each year, with the amount against each activity head.
  3. 3. Copies of any audit objections, para-wise, raised on the utilisation of CAMPA funds since 2017-18, together with the departmental replies furnished to each.
  4. 4. The action taken on the findings of CAG Report No. 5 of 2024 regarding diversion of Rs 13.86 crore to inadmissible activities, including the amount recovered, if any, and from whom.
  5. 5. The area on which compensatory afforestation was carried out and the survival rate of planted saplings recorded in each year since 2017-18, division-wise.
  6. 6. The number of proposals for diversion of forest land approved in the State, year-wise since 2017, and the area of forest land diverted under each.
  7. 7. Copies of the conditions imposed in each such approval, and the record of compliance verification carried out against those conditions.
RTI-10, महिला सुरक्षा

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Uttarakhand Police Headquarters, Dehradun, and separately, Department of Women Empowerment & Child Development

  1. 1. The number of cases registered under crimes against women in the State, district-wise, in each year since 2017, and the number in which a charge sheet was filed.
  2. 2. The number of such cases in which trial concluded, and the number resulting in conviction, year-wise since 2017.
  3. 3. The number of women's help desks and women's police stations functional in the State, district-wise, and the sanctioned versus filled strength of women police personnel.
  4. 4. The number of live-in relationships registered under the Uniform Civil Code since it came into force, month-wise, and the number of registrations of termination.
  5. 5. The number of prosecutions initiated for failure to register a live-in relationship under the said Code.
  6. 6. The number of hotels, resorts, dhabas and homestays inspected for compliance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, year-wise since 2017, and the number found non-compliant.
RTI-11, यात्रा

किसे भेजें: Public Information Officer, Department of Tourism, Government of Uttarakhand, Dehradun, and separately, the District Magistrates of Rudraprayag, Chamoli and Uttarkashi

  1. 1. Copies of any carrying-capacity study commissioned or received by the Department in respect of the Char Dham yatra routes and destination towns, together with the date of each.
  2. 2. The number of pilgrims recorded at each of the four dhams, year-wise since 2017.
  3. 3. The number of deaths recorded during the Char Dham yatra, year-wise since 2017, with cause of death where recorded.
  4. 4. The number of additional doctors, ambulances and temporary medical posts deployed on the yatra routes in each year since 2017, and the peak-week deployment separately.
  5. 5. The revenue accruing to the State from the yatra, year-wise since 2017, and the amount transferred to local bodies in the yatra districts.
  6. 6. The number of registered homestays in the State, district-wise, and the number recorded as operational in the most recent year.

No reply in 30 days, or an unsatisfactory reply, triggers a First Appeal to the First Appellate Authority in the same department, free, and due within 30 days of the deadline lapsing. Send us refusals too. A refusal is itself content.

Filing an RTI puts your name and address on record with the department. Consider that before filing on politically sensitive subjects. We will never publish a filer's identity without explicit permission.
